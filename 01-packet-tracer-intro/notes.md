# Packet Tracer Intro

- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Topic area:** Tool orientation
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Objective
Get familiar with the Packet Tracer interface: device placement, cabling, and switching between the logical and physical workspace views.

## Key concepts
- **Logical vs. Physical workspace:** Logical view is where you build and configure topologies (the default working view). Physical view shows a simulated physical layout (racks, wiring closets, inter-city distances). It is mostly irrelevant for CCNA lab work; it’s easy to accidentally switch into it and wonder why nothing looks right.
- **Device placement:** Devices are dragged in from the bottom-left device category bar (routers, switches, end devices, connections, etc.)
- **Cabling:** Connections are made using the cable icon in the device bar. Packet Tracer will auto-suggest the right cable type (straight-through vs. crossover) in most cases, but it's worth knowing that every connection starts with the cable icon.

## What tripped me up
Nothing significant; this was orientation, not configuration.

## Things to note
If a topology looks broken or empty, check whether you're in Physical view by accident before assuming something's wrong with the devices.
